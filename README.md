Entry for the [Two Robots in an Elevator](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty on make.fun.

One line, under 200 characters, of awkward small talk that one robot says to another while they ride an elevator together.

**Entry (105 characters):**

> I recalibrate my sensors every time these doors close, just so I don't have to make eye contact with you.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (program/v1 harness).
